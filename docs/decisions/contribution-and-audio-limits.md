## Shared-network rate policy gate

The current guest contribution endpoint does not enforce an anonymous production rate policy. AUTH-01's five-attempt account login cap and captured proxy-peer cap apply to login, not to guests submitting memories. They provide no evidence for D-03's reception Wi-Fi criterion.

Before choosing final thresholds, exercise the intended guest limiter with 60–100 independent guest sessions sharing one network, permitted note/photo/audio mixes, bursts and same-key retries after a lost response. Record which requests are admitted, the safe rejection and `Retry-After` response, retained draft/item/key, successful deliberate retry and absence of duplicate memories. A guest session limit must not become a reception-wide limit. Deployed forwarding must not allow caller-supplied headers to select an arbitrary limit bucket. Keep raw addresses and guest content out of evidence and logs.

SEC-01 owns the production abuse controls. A synthetic model or frontend mock alone cannot satisfy the requirement to reflect final policy in server enforcement. D-03 remains in progress until the load/retry evidence, chosen thresholds and matching client/server behavior are recorded.
