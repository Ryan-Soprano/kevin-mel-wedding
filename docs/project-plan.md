The agreed product scope is:

- The wedding release collects **written messages, photos, and audio messages** from approximately **60–100 guests**, without guest accounts. Audio remains required unless Ryan explicitly changes that scope.
- Guest names are optional. Every durable guest contribution enters a private review inbox as `PENDING` and `COUPLE_ONLY`; authenticated couple-created memories start in `KEEP` and `COUPLE_ONLY`. Nothing is automatically published.
- Kevin and Melanie are the intended private application users and product collaborators. Ryan confirmed on October 9 that they already know about the project; involve them now in design, priorities, synthetic guest testing and private-inbox usability so the archive meets their needs and supports continued use. Real account provisioning and access activation remain deliberate operator steps.
- Preserve original media and useful metadata. Privacy, reliable collection, recovery, export, and long-term ownership are core requirements. Ryan permanently owns and operates the infrastructure; Kevin and Melanie alone approve or reject submissions and decide what is published.
- This is a potential lifelong archive shaped with Kevin and Melanie. A general event website builder, RSVP system, travel planner, or commercial platform is outside committed scope.
- The learning stack is React with JavaScript and Java with Spring Boot and Maven.
